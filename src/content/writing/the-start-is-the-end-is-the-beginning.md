To arrive at something is to leave something. Such is Life.   
  
I have no real explanation for anything. Simply stories I can share with you. Even in those stories, I am not perfect. I sin.   
  
There's only so much I can do about that. I do try my best more than not these days. But I come from a land far away in some respects. "Liminal spaces made flesh" would be a romantic way to phrase it perhaps.   
  
The 21st century in the West is one of rapid developments and degradations. I have crossed many boundaries to arrive here leaving things we'll likely never discuss.  
  
A new Start for me. An End for me. A Beginning. Such is Life.
