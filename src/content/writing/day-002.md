I've been fortunate enough to be part of teams that generated $100s of millions from our brand work and executive strategy. Spanning financial technology, consumer software, and the athletic-hobbyist-lifestyle spectrum. There's more in common than not.   
  
Turning toward a **personal** brand, "*Jeremy Alexander*," I see how easily Artists are disgruntled or disillusioned with *commoditizing themselves*. 

In my opinion it does not need to be so extractive and disrespectful to ones principles and ethics. With tradeoffs of course.  
  
Hello, I am Jeremy Alexander. Nice to virtually meet you. 

Like alluded to earlier, my past is in fintech, start ups, and grassroots indie artist collectives. Copywriting, executive strategy, public facing comms, digital marketing, software design, and visual media production being some of the tools sharpened most these past 6 or so odd years.

Taking those tools for my own pursuits, I am now "making things and documenting it." But first, I need to get a brand framework/kit stood up, hence these posts. Godspeed.
